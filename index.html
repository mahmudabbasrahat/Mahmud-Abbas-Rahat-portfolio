About Me

I'm Mahmud Abbas Rahat, a multidisciplinary digital professional based in Bangladesh, working across digital marketing, SEO, graphic design and web development. I like sitting at the intersection of these four disciplines rather than picking just one — a campaign needs a strategy, a strategy needs to be found through search, and both need a visual identity and a website that hold up.

My design work spans restaurant menus, brand identity, business cards, packaging mockups and apparel graphics, built with an eye for clean layout, legible hierarchy and a consistent visual system. Alongside design, I study and apply SEO and digital marketing fundamentals, and build responsive websites using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

I approach every project the same way: understand the goal first, then choose the right mix of strategy, creativity and technical execution to reach it.

  • CreativityVisual thinking grounded in a clear brief
  • StrategyDecisions backed by research, not guesswork
  • Technical understandingComfortable across design and code
  • Continuous learningActively building skills across all four disciplines
